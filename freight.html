AProduct information

Do not know the volume? Carton calculator + loading calculator (tap to open) - which container it fits, and the utilization

Loading algorithm: cartons are test-packed in all 6 orientations (rotating L/W/H), taking the orientation with the highest carton count, then constrained by both usable volume and the container payload limit; usable volume follows practical figures of 28 CBM for 20GP / 58 CBM for 40GP / 68 CBM for 40HQ (net internal volume still allows for pallets, gaps and door clearance).
