About Yinghuo

About Us

Yiwu Yinghuo International Trade Co., Ltd. was founded in 2024 and is headquartered in Zijin Fifth District, Futian, Yiwu — a stone’s throw from Yiwu International Trade City.

We do two things. First, on-the-ground verification for overseas buyers — is the supplier real, do the goods match the order, is the factory producing as agreed? Our people go there, take photos and report back. Second, we digitize the Yiwu market — 46,354 shops and 196,652 product listings from YiwuGou, compiled into data packs by 21 categories, so overseas clients can browse all of Yiwu Trade City from home.

No category limits: construction machinery, Yiwu small commodities, new energy and energy storage are all in scope, alongside factory audits and goods inspection for overseas clients. The company is a registered vendor with the United Nations Global Marketplace (UNGM), vendor no. 1265762, and submitted an EOI in October 2026 for the WFP Iraq Healthy Kitchen and Bakery project.

1

Ground team, on-site verification

Every verification and inspection is done on-site by our own people — never a retelling of the supplier’s own claims.

2

Facts only — no self-issued certificates

We do verification, sampling and document coordination; test results and certifications come from qualified third-party bodies.

3

Verify first, pay after

Our standing advice to overseas buyers: run one independent verification before paying any new supplier.
